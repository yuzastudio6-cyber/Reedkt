# Living Frame Chat Plan Presentation

`living-frame-chat-plan-presentation-v1` is a namespaced, byte-free,
non-executable supplemental descriptor for the existing source-led edit-plan
presentation path. It is not a second chat card, approval flow, browser
rehydration owner, or plan source of truth.

## Canonical inputs

The compiler revalidates and digest-binds:

- the complete `ProfessionalSkillPlan` and its one selected Living Frame
  parent when Living Frame is selected;
- the exact `LivingFrameProfessionalSkillComponent`, or its absence for
  deliberate non-use and restraint;
- the selection-policy decision;
- the owner-scope amendment and active Professional Skill policy projection;
- the confirmed output frame and its Living Frame expectation;
- MasterTiming;
- the exact plan version; and
- either the exact approved-snapshot attachment or the explicit pending state.

The descriptor cannot be compiled from raw chat, browser-local state, tool or
provider names, media bytes, paths, URLs, commands, credentials, or caller
execution choices.

## Three presentation depths

Guided mode gives a compact selected, deferred, non-use, or restraint summary.
Detailed mode reports modes, scenes, attention, Semantic Scale, depth,
caption protection, fallback, QA, and reinspection requirements while keeping
advanced detail collapsed. Developer mode contains only opaque versioned and
digest-bound references; it does not expose internal execution details.

The descriptor remains in `phase = plan`, uses `user_summary` unless an actual
blocking state exists, and provides no separate approval call to action. The
normal complete edit-plan and credit-estimate approval remains the only
approval path.

## Reload and revision

The compiler distinguishes:

- an initial projection;
- an exact canonical reload;
- attachment of an approved snapshot to the exact unchanged plan; and
- invalidation by a later revision.

Changes to the confirmed frame, MasterTiming, owner scope, active policy,
selection, Living Frame component, or Professional Skill plan make the prior
presentation stale. Aspect-, timing-, scope-, or policy-affecting revisions
require a new plan version, reset approval and preview state, and require a new
approved snapshot. A browser-local copy cannot authorize reload.

## Current boundary

`sharedUiMutated` is `false` and `canonicalConsumptionPending` is `true`.
`ChatNativeEditor`, `getChatPlanningCards`, `InlineEditPlanCard`, and shared
presentation/rehydration owners are intentionally unchanged on this feature
branch.

At the later canonical one-writer boundary, the existing plan presentation
read model should be versioned once, consume this descriptor as one
supplemental Living Frame projection, persist and reload it from canonical
state, and prove revision invalidation plus approved-snapshot digest matching.
No separate card registry or approval owner should be introduced.

## Evidence

The focused smoke covers selected Living A-Roll, explicit restraint, approved
snapshot attachment, exact reload, confirmed-frame revision invalidation, 12
input-tamper cases, and 13 output-tamper cases. Separate signed prior-state
fixtures prove frame, MasterTiming, owner-scope, and active-policy changes each
invalidate the old presentation and reset approval. The suite also verifies
that every tool, provider, runtime, dispatch, cost, QA-approval,
public-delivery, and production authority remains false.
