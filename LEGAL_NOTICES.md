# Legal Notices

## User Audio Rights

Only upload, separate, export, or share audio that you own, have licensed, or
are legally permitted to use. Dukira does not grant rights to third-party songs,
vocals, instrumentals, or separated stems.

## Audio AI Tool Boundaries

DeepFilterNet is used for speech cleanup workflows. Demucs is used only for
vocal/music/stem separation workflows after model approval gates pass. RNNoise
is not part of the active product routing.

Generated stems, review previews, and source-derived audio remain private
artifacts unless a later reviewed export/delivery workflow explicitly approves
sharing.
