## Required evidence

For one exact approved output the record requires:

- actual uploaded-source pixels rather than synthetic engineering media;
- every rendered frame represented exactly once in ordered contact sheets;
- original-resolution checks across entries, transitions, holds, exits, and
  the tail;
- no face or gesture obstruction, clipping, overflow, inaccessible reading
  state, important source-text collision, unstable placement, unusable cue
  transition, tail truncation, or unprofessional visual treatment;
- accepted Caption-owned professional appearance;
- create-only persistence and exact reread.

This evidence complements but never replaces deterministic technical QA,
qualified complete-time shared postrender review, or independent final QA.
Caption receives no provider, repair, asset, final-QA, billing, delivery, or
production authority.
