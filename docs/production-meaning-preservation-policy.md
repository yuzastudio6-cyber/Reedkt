# Production Meaning Preservation Policy

Smart cut planning must preserve story, context, and emotional intent. Milestone 8 emits warnings and blockers, not final factual judgments.

Protected ranges include hooks, setup/context, payoff/explanation, emotional pauses, and repeated-take keepers. Removal candidates near key context words or early hook/setup regions are flagged for review.

The policy prevents accidental all-content removal and warns when a cut could alter meaning. Future human review or approved snapshot rules decide whether risky cuts may proceed.
