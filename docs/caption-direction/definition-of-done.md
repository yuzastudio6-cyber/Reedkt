# Captions Specialist Definition of Done

Terminal status for this Goal:
`caption_specialist_private_internal_qualified`.

This is not a claim that the whole ReeditPro SaaS is publicly production-ready.
It means the Captions Specialist is complete inside its boundary, privately
qualified with real media, and ready for dependency-safe integration by the
canonical backend workflow pipeline and future HQ/Orchestra.

## Required specialist completion

- `captions` exposes the approved capability manifest and per-job
  qualification snapshot.
- bounded video, scene, and boundary jobs execute through the standalone
  harness and reject peers, cycles, scope expansion, missing qualification, and
  unsupported work.
- `caption_design` assembles only necessary mini skills and
  `no_captions` is an exclusive restraint.
- one canonical transcript drives all creative, accessible, localized, and
  delivery projections with source-word and transformation lineage.
- sensitive text and timing provenance gates work; synthetic timing cannot
  drive final word motion.
- early strategy/opportunity/reservation and late picture-lock choreography are
  implemented with staleness/invalidation.
- Visual Intelligence and Track All support are consumed through typed public
  boundaries without direct Qwen or SAM ownership.
- semantic phrase, typography role, multi-font, optical size, color,
  legibility, multi-track, depth, occlusion, anchor, hero, list, B-roll,
  handoff, camera-intent, motion, reduced-motion, and Sound-support behavior
  work.
- StoryTiming owns every executable frame.
- Remotion creative, libass stable, SRT/WebVTT, multilingual/accessibility, and
  FFmpeg package paths work at exact confirmed output frames.
- approved snapshots, work/asset lineage, idempotency, private tenancy,
  persistence/reread/reconciliation, estimate/credit inputs, and observability
  connect through existing owners.
- QA covers transcript, alignment, semantic, typography, placement, occlusion,
  mask, motion, sound, accessibility, color, render, and export.
- deterministic technical QA and direct visual inspection both pass for the
  claim being made.
- local repairs and declared fallbacks are scoped, versioned, and re-inspected.
- legacy IDs/styles/plans/snapshots and the simple overlay route remain
  compatible until CAP-19 retirement fixtures pass.
- CAP-18 real-media matrix and CAP-20 full regressions pass on a reproducible
  published dependency chain.

## Honest remaining gates

The final release manifest distinguishes:

- qualified Caption-owned jobs;
- qualified injected dependency fixtures;
- missing shared-owner integration;
- external model/font/license/deployment gates;
- public/billing/production gates.

No source-only contract, historical untracked result, bounded fixture, or
technical-only raster check may be relabeled as full private qualification.

## CAP-00R acceptance

CAP-00R passes when:

- historical CAP-00 docs are present with this amendment;
- the clean base and dirty preservation paths are recorded exactly;
- owner/public-boundary, gap, duplicate-lane, keep/adapt/retire, migration,
  compatibility, and CAP-00R evidence documents exist;
- CAP-00R points to the CAP-00R–20 roadmap;
- links, prohibited stale claims, Git diff scope, and repository instructions
  pass;
- only documentation changed and no runtime/provider/media/migration/billing/
  secret/deployment action occurred;
- the checkpoint is committed and pushed.

Passing CAP-00R authorizes automatic continuation to CAP-01. It does not grant
execution, provider, billing, QA approval, public delivery, or production
authority.
