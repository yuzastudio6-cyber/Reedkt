# Edit Level Estimates By Level

## Normal

Normal estimates are the lowest mock/local band: 20-45 minutes, 1.0x multiplier, one analysis pass, targeted Qwen planning policy, targeted visual understanding only when needed, baseline QA, and future render/revision/variant budget of 1/1/1.

Normal is a clean professional edit, not a low-quality edit.

## Premium

Premium estimates use a medium mock/local band: 45-90 minutes, 2.0x multiplier, two analysis passes, two-pass future Qwen planning policy, key-moment visual understanding policy, stronger creative QA, and future render/revision/variant budget of 2/2/2.

Premium can show degraded capability warnings when key visual, transcript, or audio tools are unavailable.

## Ultra Premium

Ultra Premium estimates use the highest mock/local band: 90-180 minutes, 4.0x multiplier, three analysis passes, multi-pass future Qwen planning policy, scene-level visual understanding policy, strict studio QA, and future render/revision/variant budget of 3/3/3.

Ultra Premium can degrade to Premium-safe fallback copy when studio-level source, audio, graphic, render, or QA capabilities are missing.
