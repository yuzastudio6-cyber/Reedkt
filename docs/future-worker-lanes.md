# Future Worker Lanes

This document defines future worker lanes without implementing workers. All lanes remain blocked until their backend services, gates, storage, idempotency, readiness, compliance, and validation milestones are complete.

| Worker lane | Purpose | Required approved records | Required inputs | Expected outputs | Tool/runtime dependencies | Credit requirement | QA requirement | Retry behavior | Idempotency requirements | Blocked for now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `media_probe_worker` | Probe uploaded source media facts. | Upload intent completion, storage object record, job claim. | Canonical source media bucket/path. | Duration, codec, frame size, frame rate, readiness status, sanitized events. | FFprobe/FFmpeg readiness; private storage access. | Usually no user credit unless policy changes. | Validate probe completeness and unsupported formats. | Retry transient storage/runtime failures; terminal unsupported media. | One probe result per storage object/version/job key. | Production FFmpeg/legal/storage validation. |
| `transcript_alignment_worker` | Produce transcript and word/segment timing. | Media readiness, approved planning job, job claim. | Source audio/media storage refs, language hints. | Transcript records, transcript segments, alignment confidence. | Speech model/runtime approved in future. | Credit-bearing if policy marks transcript work billable. | Check confidence, language, speech gaps, timing sanity. | Retry transient model/runtime errors; user review for low confidence. | Idempotency by media asset version and model config. | Real speech runtime and privacy review. |
| `visual_observation_worker` | Extract visual observations for planning/QA. | Media readiness, approved planning job, job claim. | Source media refs, frame sampling policy. | Visual observation records, scene/object/safety summaries. | Future VLM/CV runtime. | Credit-bearing when model/tool use is billable. | Check source truth, privacy, and confidence. | Retry transient runtime errors; block unsupported media. | Idempotency by media asset version, sampling policy, model. | Real visual model/tool approval. |
| `audio_observation_worker` | Analyze audio clarity, loudness, beats, and issues. | Media readiness, approved planning/timing job, job claim. | Source audio/media refs. | Audio observation records, beat/loudness/readiness summaries. | Future AudioFlux/FFmpeg/audio tools after review. | Credit-bearing when analysis is billable. | Speech clarity and timing sanity checks. | Retry transient runtime errors; fallback to simpler timing if approved. | Idempotency by media version and analysis profile. | Audio tool/legal/runtime readiness. |
| `remotion_render_worker` | Compose preview/final frames from approved render manifest. | Approved snapshot, render job, render manifest, storage refs, job claim. | Render manifest, approved timing/frame context, assets. | Preview/final render artifact records, render events. | Remotion runtime, Node worker profile, private storage. | Credit reservation required for render/export where billable. | Render QA, frame/aspect/timing checks, missing asset checks. | Retry transient render/runtime errors; no plan changes. | Idempotency by render job, manifest hash, output variant. | Production render worker and storage handoff. |
| `ffmpeg_postprocess_worker` | Postprocess renders for codec, audio, captions, export packaging. | Approved snapshot, render output, export/render job, job claim. | Render artifact refs, audio/caption/format settings. | Postprocessed artifact, export candidate, logs/events. | FFmpeg LGPL-safe config, libass where needed. | Credit reservation required when billable. | Codec/playback, loudness, captions, duration, privacy checks. | Retry transient encoding/storage failures; block unsupported codec. | Idempotency by input artifact hash and export profile. | FFmpeg/legal/dependency review. |
| `image_asset_worker` | Generate or process still/keyframe/card/image assets. | Approved snapshot, generation request, credit reservation, job claim. | Prompt brief, source refs, style/frame constraints. | Generated asset records, private storage objects, provider/tool attempt records. | Provider gateway or approved deterministic image tools. | Credit reservation required for generation. | Asset QA, prompt-policy, frame/background, source truth checks. | Retry only within approved provider/fallback policy. | Idempotency by generation request and prompt hash. | Provider/tool gateway implementation. |
| `browser_capture_worker` | Capture authorized browser/screen evidence assets. | Approved snapshot, tool call intent, job claim, user authorization. | URL/capture policy, auth constraints, viewport/frame specs. | Browser capture asset records, QA notes, storage objects. | Playwright worker runtime after compliance approval. | Credit reservation if capture/processing is billable. | Privacy, robots/rate-limit, label readability, safe-zone checks. | Retry transient navigation/runtime failures; block auth/paywall/CAPTCHA bypass. | Idempotency by capture intent, URL hash, viewport, timestamp policy. | Browser capture policy and runtime approval. |
| `map_render_worker` | Render exact map/location visuals. | Approved snapshot, map plan/tool intent, job claim. | Location data, confidence labels, map style, frame safe zones. | Map asset records, QA report, storage objects. | Controlled map renderer/API after license review. | Credit reservation if billable. | Geography confidence, label readability, caption collision checks. | Retry transient tile/runtime failures; user review for uncertain location. | Idempotency by map plan, data hash, style/version. | Map tool/provider/license review. |
| `chart_render_worker` | Render exact charts, diagrams, timelines, and data graphics. | Approved snapshot, chart/tool intent, job claim. | Structured data, labels, chart settings, frame rules. | Chart/diagram asset records, QA report, storage objects. | Controlled chart/dataviz renderer. | Credit reservation if billable. | Data/source confidence, label readability, safe-zone checks. | Retry transient renderer failures; block unverified critical data if required. | Idempotency by data hash, settings, plan ID. | Dataviz tool review and source-certainty policy. |
| `tool_execution_worker` | Execute approved deterministic tools not covered by a specialized lane. | Approved snapshot, tool call intent, readiness/compliance pass, job claim. | Tool profile, canonical input refs, allowed settings. | Tool output assets, sanitized logs, QA requirements. | Approved tool runtime profile. | Credit reservation when billable. | Tool-specific QA and artifact validation. | Retry transient failures; no arbitrary fallback outside approved policy. | Idempotency by tool intent, input hash, settings hash. | Tool-call foundation, compliance, sandboxing. |
| `provider_asset_worker` | Request AI/video/image/audio assets through provider gateway. | Approved snapshot, generation request, provider readiness, credit reservation, job claim. | Provider route, prompt brief, model/tier policy, storage refs. | Provider attempt, generated asset, sanitized events. | Provider gateway, Secret Manager, private storage. | Credit reservation required. | Provider output QA and asset reconciliation. | Retry only within approved provider/fallback policy. | Idempotency by provider request attempt and prompt/model hash. | Real provider gateway and secret handling. |
| `sfx_worker` | Generate or select SFX assets and mix/trim plans. | Approved snapshot, SFX plan, credit reservation if generated, job claim. | SFX prompt/route, timing cue, mix constraints. | SFX asset records, trim/alignment/mix/QA records. | Future Mirelo/MMAudio/internal library gateway. | Required for generated SFX; library-only may differ by policy. | Voice-first mix, cue alignment, loudness, provenance. | Retry provider failures within approved fallback policy. | Idempotency by SFX event/route/prompt hash. | Real SFX provider execution. |
| `music_worker` | Generate or prepare music bed assets. | Approved snapshot, music plan, credit reservation if generated, job claim. | Music prompt/cue sheet, duration, ducking constraints. | Music asset, mix plan, QA report, storage object. | Future Lyria/provider gateway or licensed library path. | Required for generated music. | Voice clarity, loop/length, licensing/provenance. | Retry within approved music route/fallback policy. | Idempotency by music request and prompt/settings hash. | Real music provider/library runtime. |
| `qa_worker` | Run automated and structured QA checks. | Approved snapshot, target artifact/job, job claim. | Render/assets/plans/timing/storage refs. | QA report, QA issues, blocker status. | QA tools/models as approved. | Usually included in operation estimate. | Required checks depend on artifact type and tier. | Retry transient QA runtime failures; unresolved blockers require review/fix. | Idempotency by artifact version and QA profile. | Real QA tool/runtime implementation. |
| `export_worker` | Finalize private export artifacts. | Approved snapshot, final render, QA pass, export job, credit reservation if required, job claim. | Final render artifact, export profile, storage target. | Export records, export variants, storage objects, audit events. | FFmpeg/storage/runtime profile as approved. | Credit reservation required when export is billable. | Playback, duration, privacy, QA blocker check. | Retry transient encode/storage failures; block on QA/revision. | Idempotency by export job, render artifact hash, profile. | Export readiness, storage, FFmpeg/legal review. |
