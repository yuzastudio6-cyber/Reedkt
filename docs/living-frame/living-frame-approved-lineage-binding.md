# Living Frame approved lineage binding

Status: controlled, non-executable integration contract.

This slice proves how a generic Living Frame renderer/choreography candidate
must be related to ReeditPro's existing immutable approved snapshot, approved
work graph, and approved asset manifest. It creates none of those authorities.

Musashi, a helicopter, and the Strait of Hormuz remain examples used to exercise
character illustration, mechanical selective motion, and spatial/data
explanation. The binding contains no subject-specific routing.

## Existing authorities preserved

- `edit-planning-authority-service` remains the only approval and immutable
  snapshot authority.
- The approved work items remain the only work-graph authority.
- `private-edit-asset-manifest-v1` remains the only approved asset manifest.
- `MasterTimingPlan` and SoundSync remain the timing and sound authorities.
- The existing `RendererCompositionPlan` remains the renderer plan authority.
- Remotion execution, tool dispatch, QA, and private review remain closed.

The reader is registered in-process and accepts only a controlled,
non-promotable fixture source in this version. Browser or caller JSON cannot
claim that a snapshot was re-read.

## What the binding verifies

The contract independently reconstructs:

- the approved snapshot hash;
- the plan and work-graph hashes;
- the timing-component hash;
- the approved asset-manifest hash and counts;
- the exact renderer-plan component reference and content digest;
- renderer/choreography scene, timing, and output-frame agreement;
- one unambiguous approved work output per projected renderer layer;
- one matching planned asset-manifest entry per covered output.

The output is still blocked. Canonical planning must later admit and freeze the
selected Living Frame scene, renderer binding, and choreography binding as
source-bound canonical components. The existing renderer layer schema must
also be extended through its owner before Living Frame tracks, pivots, masks,
depth, camera, and local-contrast primitives can execute.

## Non-authority

Even a digest-valid all-green controlled packet cannot select a scene, approve
an edit, change timing, place sound cues, mutate work or assets, route a
provider/tool, dispatch a job, render Remotion, approve QA, or claim production
readiness. Those flags are literal `false` in the binding.
