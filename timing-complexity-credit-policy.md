# Timing Complexity Credit Policy

## Purpose

Timing complexity affects ReeditPro credits because timing increases planning, QA, tool, render, and future worker complexity. Credit impact should be transparent without making Basic feel low quality.

Basic remains professional with simpler timing. Pro and Premium can add deeper timing when it improves the edit.

## Complexity Levels

- `none`: no extra timing complexity.
- `simple`: clean cuts, readable captions, simple visual reveals, low SFX density, and the Basic professional baseline.
- `moderate`: keyword emphasis, several visual cues, phrase-aware transitions, light SoundSync, and basic map/chart/browser timing.
- `advanced`: many visual cues, step reveals, chart/map/browser animation timing, beat-aware transitions, SFX and ducking coordination, AI video duration planning, and deeper timing QA.
- `premium`: scene-level refinement, complex SoundSync, cinematic/emotional timing, many timed layers, high QA requirements, manual review notes, and premium fallback timing.

## Credit-Increasing Factors

- longer AI video clips
- more AI video clips
- more caption animation or emphasis words
- higher visual cue density
- chart/map/browser step reveals
- complex transitions
- more SFX cues
- beat-aware timing
- music ducking complexity
- emotional pause protection
- frame-accurate Remotion layer complexity
- timing QA complexity
- Premium manual timing review notes

## Credit-Reducing Factors

- simpler captions
- fewer emphasis words
- fewer SFX cues
- phrase cuts instead of beat cuts
- static cards instead of step reveals
- fewer visual cues
- shorter AI clips
- still/card alternatives
- simpler transitions
- no music or voice-only timing
- Basic-safe timing

## Tier Behavior

Basic timing should usually be `simple`, with `moderate` allowed when it remains restrained. Advanced or Premium timing should show lower-cost alternatives and may block approval when it no longer fits Basic.

Pro can use `moderate` or `advanced` timing when useful. Pro still never uses Veo.

Premium can use `advanced` or `premium` timing with stronger QA. Veo remains final fallback/rescue only for AI video assets, not a timing engine.

## User-Facing Explanation

Credit estimates should explain that timing complexity affects Reedit Credits when the plan includes frame-accurate caption animation, visual cues, SoundSync, transitions, SFX, music ducking, AI clip duration, and Remotion layer timing.

Lower-cost timing alternatives should remain professional: simpler captions, fewer SFX cues, phrase cuts only, shorter AI clips, static cards, simpler transitions, or voice-only timing.

No real timing worker runs before approval.
