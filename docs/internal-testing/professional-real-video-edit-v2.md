# Professional Real Video Edit V2

This acceptance path edits the complete private fixture at `Documents/test video/internal testing.MP4` through ReEditPro's upload, analysis, transcription, approval, credit reservation, caption, render, and QA boundaries.

## Creative Decision

- The story is rebuilt from nine reviewed source ranges: launch, product value, editor scale, customer capacity, creative control, call to action, feedback, and sign-off.
- False starts, repeated conjunctions, abandoned thoughts, and dead space are excluded without changing the speaker's intended claim order.
- The supplied Instagram reel is accepted only as caption-style reference evidence. Its heavy rounded type, strong dark edge, rapid phrase replacement, and selective orange emphasis are adapted with ReEditPro-owned raster assets; reference media and branding are not copied into the output.
- Four sparse top-safe product callouts support the story. No random B-roll, music, SFX, 3D, AI video, map, or chart is added because the source does not justify those systems.

## Audio Decision

The source is measured before execution. A fixed room-only interval and the first complete spoken line determine whether conservative spectral cleanup is justified. `clean_voice_denoised` is allowed only for a measured speech-to-noise-floor margin of 18 dB or less, uses 3-12 dB of spectral reduction, and requires both `audio_loudness` and `audio_naturalness` upstream QA.

The completed MP4 is independently measured against the -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBFS voice target and re-transcribed with the approved local faster-whisper model. Required story meaning must remain present and excluded false starts must remain absent.

## Boundaries

This is approved private local execution with an approved snapshot, credit reservation, idempotency, private artifacts, and no model download. It performs no provider calls, public delivery, Supabase/GCS writes, billing settlement, beta unlock, or production claim.
