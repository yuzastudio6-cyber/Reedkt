# Living Frame Owner Scope Amendment

## Current Active Outcome

Living Frame continues toward end-to-end private internal readiness, but the
illustrated-character animation lane is paused. This is a scope correction,
not a cancellation of Living Frame.

The active implementation scope is:

- Living A-Roll compositing;
- static illustrations used without character animation;
- non-character Living Still selective motion;
- Living Archive;
- Living Diagram;
- non-character Hybrid Expansion;
- maps, routes, timelines, diagrams, and data graphics;
- attention direction, Focus Handoff, Semantic Scale, and camera choreography;
- depth, occlusion, temporal masks, and contact-object-safe composition;
- environmental, editorial, and rigid non-character support motion;
- sound, StoryTiming, Caption Direction coordination, Remotion composition,
  QA, fallback, and private review.

## Paused Owner-Specification Tracks

The following work must not be admitted, dispatched, executed, or described as
active Living Frame readiness:

- illustrated-character animation;
- animation of humans, animals, plants, or other living/organic subjects;
- complete-character keypose generation;
- character-pose interpolation;
- character-frame cadence smoothing;
- any living/organic subject rig;
- mechanical-object rigging.

Static illustration assets remain available as still visual elements. Their
presence does not authorize character animation.

Mechanical rigging remains a separate future owner-specification track.
Unknown subjects never default to mechanical objects.

## Preservation Of Prior Research

Earlier character route, keypose, controlled-image, interpolation, OpenToonz,
PixiJS, and Blender contracts remain research evidence only. They do not grant
active admission. Rejected generic character-rig renders remain negative
engineering evidence and cannot satisfy professional visual QA.

The canonical generic selected-scene ComfyUI private operation request remains
unchanged. The previously proposed additive keypose admission wrapper must not
be compiled while this amendment is active. A later explicit owner
specification may define a new versioned admission family.

## Source Contract

The machine-readable source-only policy is
`living-frame-owner-scope-amendment-v1`. It grants no operation registration,
provider, dispatch, runtime, asset, QA-approval, render, cost, billing, public
delivery, or production authority.
