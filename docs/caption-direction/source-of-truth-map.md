| Export | render/export/delivery owners | provide creative and accessible tracks | separate caption exporter |

## Authority rules

- Caption Direction may own creative decisions but not the underlying facts, final timeline, cross-system renderers, mix, billing, or storage authority.
- StoryTiming frames are executable; seconds are display values.
- Approved snapshots freeze exact versions of transcript, frame, picture lock, style, scene graph, timing, masks, fonts, sound handoff, renderer, and QA policy.
- A later authority version invalidates dependent caption results according to [revision-and-invalidation.md](revision-and-invalidation.md).
