- content: product demonstration taught by an educator;
- source: mixed original product footage and screen recordings;
- story role: learner problem → workflow → verified product result;
- frame/platform: 16:9 YouTube standard;
- current instruction: preserve target demonstration order, exact labels, readable captions, and no decorative SFX.

Reference findings and transferable principles:

- place-first/personal discovery became a target-aware problem-to-result story emphasis rather than a copied travel sequence;
- meaning-led pacing, readable captions, restrained hierarchy, natural tonal intent, and purposeful B-roll remained reusable;
- broad audio energy remained transferable only where the target instruction allowed it.

Non-transferable/ignored/blocked:

- the source-specific branded card arrangement remained context only;
- reference shot order, footage, timing, captions, route, destination identity, people, marks, music, SFX, and layout were blocked;
- sound-effect guidance was additionally blocked by the target instruction.

Applied/adapted result:

- target B-roll advances the learner problem and verified product result using target-owned product footage;
- captions follow target speech and target-authored labels;
- graphics are original 16:9 target graphics;
- zero clarifications were required because target context was complete.

Do-not-copy result: `passed`.
