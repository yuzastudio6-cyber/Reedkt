# Canonical approved-run Caption creative review V2

Date: 2026-08-07

## Outcome

Caption creative appearance review now has an additive, source-bound V2/V5
contract for the canonical approved Caption+B-roll run. The frozen V1 review
contract remains unchanged and readable.

The new review lane binds:

- the immutable approved snapshot;
- the canonical approved execution package;
- the exact Caption planning projection and planning binding;
- the canonical rendered-media work binding;
- the exact B-roll owner request/result and owner-evidence lineage;
- the selected normalized B-roll artifact and derived Remotion proxy;
- canonical transcript and exact source-word lineage;
- the exact confirmed 3840x2160 at 30 fps output authority; and
- the exact MasterTiming and scene frame range.

The canonical approved-run harness derives the snapshot, package, Caption
planning, frame, scene, and MasterTiming references directly from the immutable
run. Callers cannot inject those references into the derivation step.

## Rendering boundary

The V5 composition profile is
`caption_direction_broll_owner_approved_run_scene_group_v5`. It renders only an
aspect-correct 640x360 private-review proxy at scale 1/6. Remotion remains the
canonical final-canvas owner. The creative review supplements the canonical
final composition and cannot replace it.

The request requires real selected-source pixels, full and reduced-motion
variants, exact source-word lineage, complete-time inspection, and direct
raster inspection. Synthetic engineering fixtures cannot qualify professional
appearance.
