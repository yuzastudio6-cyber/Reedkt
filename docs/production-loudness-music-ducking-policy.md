# Production Loudness Music Ducking Policy

Default loudness planning targets voice-first online delivery:

- around `-16 LUFS` for many social/web voice cases;
- around `-14 LUFS` where appropriate for premium/podcast-style delivery;
- around `-1 dBTP` true peak.

Exact delivery standards may vary by platform and later export settings.

Music must not overpower speech. Ducking is planned only when music/speech overlap exists or is explicitly provided. Voice-only plans must not add music or ducking.
