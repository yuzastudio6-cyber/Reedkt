# ReeditPro Design System & UX/UI Specification

**Document:** `design.md`  
**Product:** ReeditPro  
**Version:** 1.0 production design direction  
**Purpose:** Define a consistent, professional, production-ready design system for the ReeditPro website, desktop web app, and mobile companion app.

---

## Current Product Direction Addendum

This addendum updates product direction without replacing the design system below.

- ReeditPro is web-first. Desktop/web product work comes before mobile.
- Mobile is future only and should be treated as a companion app later, not part of the current build phase.
- The three current visual signature systems are:
  1. **Stroke Motion** - 2D overlay motion storytelling.
  2. **Graphic Design / VisualExplain** - clean explanatory graphic overlays.
  3. **Real Motion** - realistic animated overlay objects/scenes inside the user's video.
- SoundSync remains important, but it is the audio/timing support engine for music, SFX, beat timing, mood, ducking, transition sounds, and emotional polish. It is not the third visual signature system.
- StoryTiming coordinates captions, cuts, Stroke Motion, Graphic Design / VisualExplain, Real Motion, SoundSync, and story beats.
- Video type dropdown workflows provide context only. They do not automatically decide which signature systems are used.
- ReeditPro should never start expensive AI editing, rendering, or generation until the AI understands the user's goal and the user approves the edit plan and credit estimate.

## 1. Product Identity

ReeditPro is an AI-first video editing workspace built around one main promise:

> **ReeditPro turns spoken ideas into perfectly timed visual stories.**

The product is not only an AI video editor. It is a creative system that understands the speaker’s message, finds the story, then builds the edit with motion, graphics, sound, timing, and export-ready polish.

ReeditPro should be known for three signature systems:

1. **StrokeMotion AI** — transparent 2D stroke-style motion storytelling that follows what the speaker is saying.
2. **VisualExplain AI** — graphic design overlays for education, concepts, frameworks, lists, diagrams, and visual understanding.
3. **SoundSync AI** — music, sound effects, beat timing, transitions, and soundtrack mood matching.

The core difference:

- **StrokeMotion tells the story.**
- **Graphic design explains the idea.**
- **SoundSync adds emotion and polish.**
- **StoryTiming makes everything land at the right moment.**

## 3. UX Principles

Every screen and feature should follow these principles.

### 3.1 Story First

ReeditPro should not add visuals randomly. Every animation, graphic, caption, transition, and sound should support the message being spoken.

### 3.2 AI With Control

The AI should create, suggest, and automate, but the user must always be able to preview, approve, edit, regenerate, remove, or send work to the full editor.

### 3.3 Desktop Is Full Power

The website/desktop product contains the complete editing system: full timeline, project management, media library, StrokeMotion, VisualExplain, SoundSync, collaboration, review, analytics, and exports.

### 3.4 Mobile Is a Companion

The mobile app is intentionally limited. It focuses on quick uploads, quick AI edits, review, comments, approvals, export monitoring, insights, and opening the full edit on desktop.

### 3.5 Timing Is the Product

The strongest part of ReeditPro is not only generating visuals. It is timing visuals, motion, captions, music, and transitions to the speaker’s words.

### 3.6 Premium, Not Busy

The interface should feel advanced, but not overwhelming. Use dark surfaces, clear hierarchy, subtle glow, clean typography, consistent spacing, and strong alignment.

### 3.7 Transparent AI

The AI must show what it changed and why. Users should see applied edits, confidence, timing markers, story beats, and visual layers.

## 6. Brand System

### 6.1 Logo Concept

The ReeditPro mark is a stylized **R** that combines:

- **R** for ReeditPro
- **Chat bubble** for AI chat editing
- **Film strip** for video/media
- **Play triangle** for playback/video creation
- **Integrated timeline** for editing precision
- **Cyan playhead marker** for timing control

The timeline must be part of the logo mark itself. It should not sit as a separate decorative line underneath the icon. The correct brand direction is:

> The R itself contains the editing timeline.

### 6.2 Logo Variations

Required logo assets:

1. **Primary horizontal lockup** — R mark + ReeditPro wordmark.
2. **App icon** — rounded-square icon with the R mark only.
3. **Standalone mark** — R mark without icon background.
4. **Mono mark** — white or single-color version for simple use.
5. **Dark background logo** — default usage.
6. **Light background logo** — simplified version with adjusted contrast.

### 6.3 Wordmark

- Text: `ReeditPro`
- `Reedit`: white or near-white
- `Pro`: blue-to-violet gradient
- Style: modern, bold, geometric sans-serif
- Letter spacing: tight but readable
- Avoid overly futuristic fonts that reduce legibility.

### 6.4 App Icon Rules

The App Store icon should be clean and highly readable at small sizes.

Icon requirements:

- Rounded-square dark background
- Centered R mark
- No wordmark
- No small external decoration
- No separate underline timeline
- Integrated timeline inside the R
- Strong silhouette
- Limited detail
- High contrast
- Works at tiny sizes

Icon must communicate:

- AI chat
- Video editing
- Timeline precision
- Creative technology

Recommended export plan:

- Create one high-resolution master icon.
- Test at 1024px, 512px, 256px, 128px, 64px, and 32px.
- Validate final submission assets against current platform requirements before publishing.

### 6.5 Clear Space

Use the height of the R film-strip stem as the minimum clear space around the full logo.

For the app icon, keep the R mark visually centered with enough padding so the mark does not touch rounded icon edges.

### 6.6 Logo Do Not

Do not:

- Add a separate timeline below the app icon.
- Use the timeline as random decoration.
- Add too many tick marks in the icon.
- Stretch the wordmark.
- Put the logo on low-contrast backgrounds.
- Add shadows that blur the mark.
- Use different gradients for the logo across screens.
- Use the icon with unrelated colors.

## 11. Signature Creative Systems

## 11.1 StrokeMotion AI

StrokeMotion AI is ReeditPro’s signature feature. It creates transparent 2D stroke-style animations that tell the story while the speaker is talking.

### Purpose

Use StrokeMotion when the video needs to show:

- A person moving
- A journey
- A problem
- A transformation
- An emotion
- A character action
- A before/after story
- A simple metaphor
- A sequence of events

### Visual Style

StrokeMotion visuals should feel:

- Minimal
- Smooth
- Human
- Lightweight
- Transparent-background
- Timed to speech
- Easy to understand
- Not cartoonish unless user chooses playful style

### StrokeMotion Elements

- Stroke character walking
- Character thinking
- Character stuck/confused
- Character pointing
- Character building
- Character celebrating
- Arrows and paths
- Simple objects
- Minimal scene props
- Motion lines
- Transformation shapes
- Journey path lines

### Timing Rules

StrokeMotion must sync to:

- Word-level timestamps
- Sentence beats
- Pauses
- Emotional changes
- Scene transitions
- Music beats
- Emphasized words

Example:

If speaker says: “Then I started moving forward.”  
The walking motion should begin around “moving forward,” not before the sentence starts.

### Layer Rules

StrokeMotion appears as its own timeline layer:

- `StrokeMotion Track`
- Transparent background
- Editable entry/exit timing
- Adjustable style
- Regeneratable per segment
- SFX attached to motion events

### Intensity Levels

1. **Minimal** — small arrows, icons, light motion.
2. **Balanced** — simple character and visual story moments.
3. **Expressive** — stronger character motion, more transitions, more SFX.
4. **Cinematic** — advanced storytelling moments, music syncing, bigger visual beats.

## 11.2 VisualExplain AI

VisualExplain AI creates graphic design overlays that help the viewer understand ideas.

### Purpose

Use VisualExplain when the video needs to explain:

- Education
- Frameworks
- Lists
- Concepts
- Data
- Processes
- Comparisons
- Product features
- Step-by-step instructions
- Business systems
- Tutorials

### VisualExplain Elements

- Clean title cards
- Numbered lists
- Process flows
- Diagrams
- Comparison tables
- Icon cards
- Charts
- Callouts
- Product UI highlights
- Arrows
- Labels
- Framework layouts
- Before/after cards

### Difference From StrokeMotion

StrokeMotion is narrative. VisualExplain is informational.

- StrokeMotion: “A person is stuck and then moves forward.”
- VisualExplain: “There are three steps: plan, create, publish.”

### Style Rules

- Keep graphics clean and aligned.
- Do not overload the video.
- Use consistent text sizes.
- Maintain high contrast.
- Use brand colors carefully.
- Use simple diagrams instead of heavy illustration.

## 11.3 SoundSync AI

SoundSync AI matches music, sound effects, transitions, and timing to the video’s mood and story.

### Purpose

SoundSync should make edits feel emotionally correct, polished, and professional.

### SoundSync Inputs

- Transcript
- Speaker energy
- Video pacing
- Scene type
- Mood
- Story beat
- Platform
- User preference
- Visual layer timing

### SoundSync Outputs

- Background music
- Sound effects
- Risers
- Impacts
- Whooshes
- Beat cuts
- Music ducking under voice
- Transition sounds
- Motion graphic SFX
- End resolve

### Mood Tags

- Inspirational
- Energetic
- Premium
- Calm
- Educational
- Dramatic
- Playful
- Corporate
- Cinematic
- Minimal

### Story Beat Sound Rules

| Story Beat | Sound Direction |
|---|---|
| Hook | Fast rise, impact, attention hit |
| Problem | Lower mood, subtle tension |
| Explanation | Clean rhythm, non-distracting bed |
| Example | Light motion SFX, clarity |
| Transformation | Lift, build, brighter movement |
| Result | Beat hit, success cue |
| CTA | Clean ending, soft resolve |

## 11.4 StoryTiming Engine

The StoryTiming Engine coordinates:

- Captions
- StrokeMotion
- VisualExplain graphics
- Music
- Sound effects
- Timeline cuts
- Scene transitions
- Beat drops
- Speaker emphasis

### Required Story Beat Map

Every AI-generated edit should create a Story Beat Map:

1. Hook
2. Setup
3. Problem
4. Explanation
5. Example
6. Solution
7. Transformation
8. Result
9. CTA

Not every video needs all beats, but the AI should classify the structure.

### User-Facing Timing Controls

Users should be able to say:

- Move this earlier
- Move this later
- Match this word
- Match the beat
- Make animation slower
- Make it faster
- Make motion more subtle
- Make it more energetic
- Regenerate this moment
- Remove this visual

## 15. UX Flows

## 15.1 Create Project Flow

1. User clicks New Project.
2. User names project.
3. User uploads media or starts from template.
4. User selects goal:
   - Talking-head edit
   - Podcast clip
   - Product demo
   - Education video
   - Marketing video
   - Social cut
5. User selects editing level:
   - Basic
   - Pro
   - Advanced / Viral
6. User selects visual style:
   - No visual layer
   - StrokeMotion
   - VisualExplain
   - Balanced mix
7. User selects soundtrack mood.
8. AI creates first edit plan.
9. User previews and approves.

---

## 15.2 AI Edit Flow

1. User opens AI Editor.
2. User types edit request.
3. AI analyzes transcript and media.
4. AI proposes edits.
5. User previews changes.
6. User applies, modifies, or rejects.
7. Changes appear on timeline.
8. User exports or sends for review.

---

## 15.3 StrokeMotion Flow

1. AI detects story moments.
2. AI suggests stroke animations.
3. User previews each moment.
4. User chooses intensity.
5. AI syncs animation to words.
6. Animation appears on StrokeMotion track.
7. SoundSync adds SFX if enabled.
8. User can regenerate or adjust timing.

---

## 15.4 VisualExplain Flow

1. AI detects explanation moments.
2. AI identifies concept type:
   - List
   - Process
   - Comparison
   - Data
   - Framework
   - Product explanation
3. AI generates clean graphic design overlay.
4. User previews.
5. User applies or changes style.
6. Graphic appears on VisualExplain track.

---

## 15.5 SoundSync Flow

1. AI detects mood and pacing.
2. AI maps story beats.
3. AI recommends music and SFX.
4. User previews soundtrack.
5. User chooses mood/intensity.
6. AI syncs music and SFX to the timeline.
7. User exports or modifies.

---

## 15.6 Review and Approval Flow

1. Editor sends project to review.
2. Reviewer receives link or mobile notification.
3. Reviewer watches video.
4. Reviewer comments at timestamps.
5. Reviewer chooses approve or request changes.
6. Changes appear in review queue.
7. Editor applies changes.
8. New version is uploaded.
9. Final approval triggers export.

## 20. Accessibility

Accessibility must be included from the start.

### 20.1 Contrast

- Primary text must be high contrast on dark surfaces.
- Muted text should still be readable.
- Do not rely only on color for status.
- Pair colors with icons and labels.

### 20.2 Keyboard Navigation

Desktop app must support:

- Keyboard focus states
- Tab navigation
- Escape to close modals
- Command palette shortcut
- Timeline keyboard shortcuts
- Enter/space for button activation

### 20.3 Motion Sensitivity

Offer motion settings:

- Full motion
- Reduced motion
- Disable decorative animation

StrokeMotion previews should respect reduced-motion preferences.

### 20.4 Captions and Audio

- Captions should be editable.
- Voice cleanup should be reversible.
- Audio previews should have clear controls.
- Soundtrack volume must be adjustable against speech.

## 27. Final Design Direction

ReeditPro should feel like a premium AI creative operating system, not a generic video editor.

The interface should communicate:

- Speed
- Intelligence
- Creative power
- Trust
- Storytelling
- Timing precision
- Professional output

The product’s strongest identity is:

> **StrokeMotion storytelling, graphic design clarity, and soundtrack timing — all controlled by AI.**

The final product should make users feel:

```text
I can upload my video, explain what I want, and ReeditPro will understand the story, create the right visuals, match the music, and help me export a polished edit faster than I could do manually.
```

That is the ReeditPro design standard.
