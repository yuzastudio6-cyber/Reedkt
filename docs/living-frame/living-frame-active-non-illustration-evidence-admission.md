# Living Frame Active Non-Illustration Evidence Admission

## Purpose

`living-frame-active-non-illustration-evidence-admission-v1` is a byte-free,
non-executable structural admission candidate for the 12 cases in
`living-frame-active-non-illustration-aggregate-v1`.

It closes the source-interface gap between the active-case manifest and a
future canonical aggregate runner. It does not execute a case, accept a render,
persist an artifact, approve QA, assemble private review, or create a competing
readiness owner.

## Canonical Consumer Boundary

The existing canonical consumer chain remains:

1. exact approved execution package and work item;
2. final artifact persistence;
3. deterministic artifact QA;
4. artifact reconciliation;
5. `canonical-private-review-assembly-service`; and
6. `canonical-private-review-decision-service`.

The admission candidate is not a direct adapter into that chain. It records
`canonicalConsumptionPending: true`. A later canonical one-writer extension
must make accepted aggregate evidence a required supplemental QA dependency of
final-artifact reconciliation and private-review assembly.

## Exact Case Binding

Each of the 12 ordered cases binds:

- the exact aggregate-manifest case digest;
- a predeclared manifest-packet digest over the exact case, scope, identity,
  and canonical plan bindings, preventing whole-evidence substitution between
  otherwise valid cases;
- workspace, project, edit session, approved snapshot, execution package,
  selected scene, approved work item, output, and expected asset identities;
- the approved snapshot v3 and canonical execution package v5;
- MasterTiming, confirmed output frame, asset manifest, and Remotion binding;
- the exact private final Remotion MP4 identity, version, object hash, and
  content hash;
- deterministic final QA and artifact reconciliation references;
- an opaque Qwen provider-lifecycle reference plus exact postrender request,
  result, and complete-time coverage references;
- separate verified full-duration audio evidence;
- Kimi-primary or allowed Terra-fallback Head QA recommendation;
- N+1 lineage when repair occurred; and
- the canonical private-review manifest reference.

Shared lifecycle records remain opaque until their canonical response surfaces
are released. Every opaque reference requires an immutable canonical reread.

## Visual And Audio Evidence

Structural admission requires all 13 professional visual checks, complete
timeline coverage, deterministic coverage-integrity evidence, and Qwen acting
only as the visual-evidence specialist. Caller inspection assertions and
technical-metrics-only acceptance are rejected.

Audio evidence is separate from Qwen. It must cover the full duration and bind
speech, SFX, music, ducking, and narration-protection evidence. A caller audio
assertion or partial coverage is rejected.

Kimi is the primary Head QA reasoner. GPT-5.6 Terra may be used only after an
allowed classified Kimi failure. The accepted structural candidate requires an
`accept` recommendation based on verified deterministic, visual, and audio
evidence. That recommendation cannot claim canonical approval or replace user
private review.

## Repair

An unrepaired artifact carries no prior-artifact lineage. A repaired artifact
must be exactly artifact version N+1, must have a different artifact identity
and content hash, and must bind a new evidence-set digest. Reusing v1 or reusing
the prior QA/visual/audio/Head-QA evidence fails closed.

The future canonical consumer must still reread and verify the referenced
artifact and every evidence dependency. The source candidate cannot prove that
an opaque digest corresponds to accepted canonical evidence.

## Paused Scope

Illustrated or living-character animation, character keyposes, interpolation,
living-subject rigs, and mechanical rigging remain non-admissible. Historical
character or rigging evidence cannot satisfy an active case. Static
illustrations may remain present only without character animation.

## Authority

This contract grants no approved-snapshot, work-graph, asset-manifest,
provider, audio, Head-QA, dispatch, runtime, artifact, QA, reconciliation,
private-review, cost, billing, delivery, or production authority. Remotion
remains the final-canvas owner, and canonical private review remains the only
review authority.

The source contract and adversarial verifier are implemented. Canonical
