# Remediation Workstream Plan

The remediation plan splits whole-product readiness into ordered workstreams:

- Deployment and rollback.
- Model/license, security, and cost controls.
- Private storage, deletion, and Supabase/GCS policy.
- Observability, incident response, legal, support, and operations.
- Backend/database, billing, and production credit ledger.
- Real generation/export worker E2E proof.
- Public artifact, signed URL, and delivery policy.
- External beta go/no-go.
