# Living Frame Non-Illustration Active Gap Audit

## Decision

The historical Living Frame private aggregate is no longer the active
completion definition. It includes illustrated-character animation, keypose,
interpolation, and rigging cases that the owner has paused. Those cases remain
research evidence but cannot make the amended goal green.

The machine-readable audit is
`living-frame-non-illustration-readiness-audit-v3`.

## Current Strengths

The active scope already has verified source contracts for:

- the composite parent skill, all five modes, and deliberate non-use;
- semantic scene reasoning, selection, and restraint;
- confirmed-frame, MasterTiming, selected-scene, approved-lineage, work-graph,
  asset-manifest, and Remotion ownership boundaries;
- attention, Focus Handoff, Semantic Scale, camera, depth, caption priority,
  environmental motion, and fallback planning; and
- exact routing to existing map, dataviz, Caption Direction, SoundSync, mask,
  approved-snapshot, renderer, QA, and private-review owners.
- a general non-character professional-review gate that separates technical
  execution from visual acceptance, requires full playback and sampled-frame
  inspection, and blocks downstream use until repair/reinspection succeeds.
- canonical motion v3 source derivation that assigns layer/source/camera tracks
  by component role and linked activation, protects static/source/mask layers,
  and preserves literal map/data scale.

Recorded private fixtures cover all five modes, confirmed portrait/custom
frames, a safe-space A-roll fallback, deterministic diagram reveal, archive
parallax, hybrid expansion, non-character selective motion, procedural
particles, caption priority, and one restrained sound cue. Those records must
be reinspected under the new general scene-review contract before they can be
called professionally accepted.

## Active Blocking Gaps

1. Reconcile canonical motion v3 at the backend one-writer boundary and bind
   its digest through admitted work, manifest, renderer, QA, and review. The
   feature source and active A-roll regression are green; shared canonical
   consumption is not yet complete.
2. Bind semantic sound triggers into canonical StoryTiming and SoundSync with
   exact attention identity, motion lineage, hit, envelope, asset, mix,
   narration protection, snapshot, work, and review evidence.
3. Close advanced temporal Living A-Roll masking through the hardened SAM2
   contract, real L4 evidence, create-only persistence, and measured edge,
   temporal, coverage, and contact-object QA. Keep safe-space fallback active.
4. Reconcile procedural-timeline artifacts into the canonical generic scene
   evidence and private-review owners.
5. Add exact source-bound map/route, readable archive-document, exact diagram,
   and non-character hybrid fixtures with complete snapshot → work → asset →
   timing → renderer → QA → review lineage.
6. Reconcile the already-frozen Caption Direction public adapter at the
   canonical one-writer boundary while preserving separate ownership.
7. Replace the historical 39/40-case completion signal with an active
   non-illustration aggregate that fails if any paused evidence is counted.
8. Render representative non-illustration scenes, inspect every complete clip
   at playback speed plus required sampled frames, and repair or reject every
   result that misses the professional bar.

## Deferred, Not Blocking The Active Goal

- illustrated-character animation;
- animation of living or organic subjects;
- complete-character keypose generation;
- ToonCrafter character interpolation;
- RIFE character cadence smoothing;
- AuraFace character-continuity measurement for the paused route;
- Blender/OpenToonz living-subject rigs; and
- mechanical-object rigging pending its separate owner specification.

Simple rigid non-character transforms and environmental/editorial motion are
not the future mechanical-rig system. They remain active only when they use an
already-approved deterministic component transform and do not infer joints,
constraints, hidden geometry, or a rig.

## Implementation Order

1. Attention, Semantic Scale, camera, and exact SoundSync integration while the
   canonical owner reconciles motion v3.
2. Temporal A-roll masking plus fallback parity.
3. Exact map/archive/diagram/hybrid work and artifact lineage.
4. Caption boundary reconciliation.
5. Active-scope aggregate.
6. Representative renders, Head Intelligence inspection, local repair, and
   final private internal review.

No step grants provider, dispatch, billing, public-delivery, or production
authority.
