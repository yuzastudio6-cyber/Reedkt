# Phase 46D Private Artifact Policy

Private reads are limited to JSON metadata artifacts under the exact Phase 46B and Phase 46C QA prefixes.

Private uploads are limited to metadata-only Phase 46D QA reports under `gs://reeditpro-staging-reeditpro-qa-artifacts/activation/phase46d/reporting-qa-integration/<run-id>/`.

Media files, frames, thumbnails, model files, secrets, signed URLs, provider logs, and public artifacts are not allowed.
