# Production SoundSync Foundation Policy

Milestone 9 SoundSync creates timing cue metadata only.

Cue sources may include timeline clips, caption segments, smart cut decisions, silence/emotional pauses, energy placeholders, and visual cue references. Cue types include cut, caption emphasis, visual reveal, transition, SFX hit, music duck, beat marker, emotional pause, card reveal, and custom.

No beat detection is claimed unless actual analysis has run. No SFX generation, music generation, provider call, or final audio mix occurs in Milestone 9.
