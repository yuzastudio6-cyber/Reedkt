## Retire from primary ownership

- flat `captions.*` definitions as the complete Caption owner;
- top-level `caption_design` callable identity;
- Caption-owned Head Intelligence or Qwen provider dispatch/result lifecycle;
- direct Caption SAM 2, SAM 3.1, or mask-model execution;
- direct peer dispatch or support recursion;
- caption-worker creative phrase/style decisions;
- synthetic/interpolated timing as final word-motion truth;
- unconditional `bottom_safe` placement;
- universal captions-above-all-masks rule for creative tracks;
- one-track-only scene model;
- fixed `1080×1920` ASS canvas as final authority;
- mutable system-family font resolution;
- full-frame PNG as the only creative renderer;
- hidden browser/local completion state;
- duplicate approval, estimate, credits, queue, lease, asset, QA, export, or
  delivery owners;
- raw divergent migrations and broad-copy publication.

No compatibility path is destructively removed before CAP-19 fixtures and
rollback evidence pass.
