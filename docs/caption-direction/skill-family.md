# Captions Specialist and Internal Skill Family

## Identity hierarchy

- Specialist key: `captions`
- Specialist display name: Captions
- Internal composite key: `caption_design`
- Internal composite display name: Caption Direction
- Compatibility alias: `caption_direction` → `caption_design`
- Restraint: `no_captions`

The specialist is the bounded job assignee and result producer. The composite
is the internal professional-skill assembly used to solve a job. Mini skills
are reusable components in the existing professional-skill catalog, not a new
taxonomy and not independently dispatched peers.

## Scene-aware assembly

The specialist selects the smallest justified component set for the assigned
scope. Activation consumes the approved request, transcript/evidence refs,
output frame, scene/boundary scope, qualification snapshot, and allowed support
routes. It must reject cycles, contradictory restraint/selection, missing
qualifications, and unbounded component activation.
