# Caption Readability + Motion Policy

## Purpose

Caption animation should improve comprehension, not distract from the edit. ReeditPro should keep captions readable, safe, and suited to the user intent, aspect ratio, category, and edit level.

## Readability Rules

Captions should stay on screen long enough, use safe word counts, avoid overlapping important visuals, and avoid animation that makes text hard to read. They should not overuse emphasis words, should respect platform and safe-zone constraints, and should adapt to the confirmed output aspect ratio.

Captions must not hide faces, mouths, product details, map pins, chart labels, browser highlight zones, source labels, or fact-safety notes. Captions stay above background visuals and masks, but they should not cover the main information.

## Caption Density by Aspect Ratio

9:16 should use shorter chunks. Captions are usually lower or center-lower, but must avoid lower visual panels and faces.

16:9 can use lower-third or subtitle-block captions. Side-by-side layouts need explicit caption zone planning.

1:1 should avoid clutter and use centered captions with a safe margin.

4:5 should use portrait feed-safe captions and avoid bottom UI or crop risk.

## Motion Rules

Basic uses fade or soft pop only and avoids heavy kinetic typography. Pro may use keyword emphasis and word highlight. Premium may use refined micro-motion, but documentary and source-safe captions remain restrained.

Motion should never make captions unreadable, cover important visuals, or fight the speaker's meaning.

## Safety Rules

Captions must stay above background visuals but below the viewer's comprehension threshold: no clutter, no random emphasis, and no collisions with face, product, map, chart, browser, source label, or legal/fact-safety areas. Captions should never sit behind foreground masks.
