# Job Worker Gate Contract

These gates must block future job, worker, provider, render, tool, media, and export execution unless they pass. Prompt 8 documents and represents the gates but does not run execution.

| Gate | Purpose | Checked by | Required records | Pass condition | Fail condition | Failure status | User-facing message style | Audit event | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `AuthGate` | Ensure caller is authenticated. | API middleware | Auth session | User context exists. | Missing/invalid session. | `blocked` | Ask user to sign in. | `auth.required` | Required before idempotency. |
| `WorkspaceGate` | Scope work to a workspace. | Backend API | `workspaces`, `workspace_members` | Caller is member. | No membership. | `blocked` | Say project is not accessible. | `workspace.access_denied` | Never leak workspace existence. |
| `ProjectAccessGate` | Scope job to a project. | `ProjectService` | `projects`, `workspace_members` | Project workspace membership is valid. | Project missing or non-member. | `blocked` | Say project is not accessible. | `project.access_denied` | Used by all read/write boundaries. |
| `ApprovedSnapshotGate` | Ensure workers execute frozen plan state. | `JobService` | `approved_plan_snapshots` | Snapshot exists and is approved. | Missing or mutable plan ref. | `blocked` | Ask user to approve the edit plan. | `job.snapshot_missing` | No raw chat execution. |
| `CreditEstimateApprovalGate` | Ensure estimate was approved. | Credit/job services | `credit_estimates`, `approval_records` | Estimate is approved. | Estimate missing/unapproved. | `blocked` | Ask user to approve estimate. | `job.credit_estimate_unapproved` | Must match snapshot scope. |
| `CreditReservationGate` | Prevent unpaid expensive work. | Credit/job services | `credit_reservations` | Active reservation covers work. | Missing/inactive reservation. | `blocked` | Ask user to reserve credits. | `job.credit_reservation_missing` | Prompt 8 does not mutate credits. |
| `JobDependencyGate` | Enforce DAG ordering. | Job service/future worker | `job_dependencies`, upstream `jobs` | Required upstream statuses pass. | Dependency missing/not complete. | `blocked` | Say waiting on dependent work. | `job.dependency_blocked` | Prevents downstream execution. |
| `JobStatusGate` | Prevent invalid transitions. | Job service/future worker | `jobs` | Current status allows action. | Terminal or incompatible status. | `blocked` | Say job cannot transition. | `job.status_blocked` | Future transaction owns mutation. |
| `JobIdempotencyGate` | Prevent duplicate writes. | Idempotency middleware | `api_idempotency_keys` | Header exists and hash matches. | Missing/conflicting key. | `blocked` | Ask caller to retry with same key. | `job.idempotency_conflict` | Existing middleware handles this. |
| `WorkerRuntimeGate` | Ensure worker lane exists and is enabled. | Worker service | runtime registry | Worker type is allowed and ready. | Unsupported or disabled lane. | `backend_required` | Say runtime is not enabled. | `worker.runtime_blocked` | Prompt 8 returns metadata only. |
| `WorkerClaimGate` | Prevent duplicate active workers. | Worker service/future RPC | `worker_job_claims`, `worker_leases` | No active claim exists. | Active claim exists or claim not transactional. | `backend_required` | Say claim requires backend runtime. | `worker.claim_blocked` | Needs transaction/unique index validation. |
| `WorkerLeaseGate` | Ensure lease token and expiry are valid. | Future worker API | `worker_leases` | Lease active and not expired. | Missing/expired/invalid token. | `blocked` | Say worker lease expired. | `worker.lease_expired` | Do not expose token. |
| `WorkerHeartbeatGate` | Keep active workers observable. | Future worker API | `worker_leases`, `worker_job_claims` | Heartbeat is timely. | Heartbeat stale/missing. | `blocked` | Say worker lost heartbeat. | `worker.heartbeat_stale` | Recovery requires idempotency review. |
| `StaleClaimRecoveryGate` | Avoid duplicate side effects after stale claims. | Worker recovery service | claims, leases, job events | Side effects are known safe. | Provider/render/tool side effects unknown. | `blocked` | Say recovery needs review. | `worker.stale_recovery_blocked` | Provider-like work requires manual/automated reconciliation. |
| `RetryBudgetGate` | Stop infinite retries. | Job retry service | `jobs`, `job_events` | Attempts below max. | Max attempts reached. | `blocked` | Say retry budget exhausted. | `job.retry_budget_exhausted` | Future policy owns retry count. |
| `CancellationGate` | Respect user/system cancellation. | Job service/future worker | `jobs`, `job_events` | Job is cancellable. | Already terminal or unsafe to cancel. | `blocked` | Say cancellation cannot be applied. | `job.cancel_blocked` | Provider/render side effects may need cleanup. |
| `ProviderExecutionBlockedGate` | Stop provider calls from job layer. | Job/worker services | provider readiness | Provider route separately approved. | Provider route requested too early. | `blocked` | Say provider execution is not enabled. | `provider.execution_blocked` | Prompt 14 owns provider gateway. |
| `RenderExecutionBlockedGate` | Stop render execution from job layer. | Job/worker services | render readiness | Render route separately approved. | Render requested too early. | `blocked` | Say render execution is not enabled. | `render.execution_blocked` | Prompt 10 owns render. |
| `ToolExecutionBlockedGate` | Stop tool execution from job layer. | Worker service | tool readiness | Tool route separately approved. | Tool requested too early. | `blocked` | Say tool execution is not enabled. | `tool.execution_blocked` | Prompt 12/13 own tools. |
| `StorageReadinessGate` | Ensure inputs are canonical private objects. | Future media/job service | `storage_object_records` | Bucket/path references are ready. | Missing storage refs. | `blocked` | Say media is not ready. | `storage.readiness_blocked` | Prompt 4 owns upload boundary. |
| `QABlockerGate` | Prevent final output with blocking QA. | Future QA/render service | `qa_reports`, `qa_check_results` | No blocking QA issues. | Blocking QA exists. | `blocked` | Say QA needs review/fix. | `qa.blocked` | Prompt 11 owns QA. |
