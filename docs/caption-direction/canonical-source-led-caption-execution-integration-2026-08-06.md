## Outcome

The normal source-led edit workflow now selects the professional Caption
specialist from the saved Edit Brief, compiles exact transcript-derived Caption
cues, publishes the specialist components and estimate into the one canonical
plan, freezes them in the immutable approved snapshot, and schedules them in
the existing backend work graph.

One bounded route proof produced 25 approved jobs. Sixteen private jobs
completed with zero execution failures. The graph then stopped exactly at:

- `canonical_caption_hq_mediated_support_resume`; and
- `canonical_caption_postrender_visual_intelligence_owner_result_read_port`.

Two jobs are capability-blocked and seven downstream jobs are dependency-
blocked. This is the intended fail-closed state because the route fixture does
not contain authenticated Visual Intelligence owner evidence. No synthetic
evidence was promoted to satisfy that owner.

## Dependency correction

Approved job records are immutable. Root Caption jobs remain `ready`, while
jobs with dependencies remain `blocked`. The worker lease owner now derives a
read-only dependency-admission record from exact persisted upstream artifact,
QA, reconciliation, and execution-fence evidence. The Caption runner accepts a
dependent job only when that record is present, digest-valid, graph-exact, and
bound to every declared dependency.

Downstream admission reopens and verifies Caption planning artifact bytes. A
missing, crossed, substituted, stale, or tampered dependency fails closed. No
job status is rewritten and no second scheduler is introduced.

## Transcript and cue authority

The test route mounts a structural source-analysis owner fixture and a Caption
transcript owner-read fixture. The resulting cue is derived from the
authenticated transcript record rather than the retired marker-text renderer.
These fixtures prove the server wiring and lineage only; they are explicitly
not real transcript or audio-truth qualification evidence.

## What the synthetic images prove

The bright color bars, blocks, and geometric shapes in the route fixture are
engineering stress content. They exercise exact frame dimensions, timing,
layer ordering, collision behavior, and readable text placement against
difficult backgrounds. They are not a design proposal for customer captions,
not a professional-appearance sample, and not AI visual review evidence.

Professional visual acceptance still requires the exact rendered Caption
outputs to be inspected through the qualified Visual Intelligence owner and
then reconciled with deterministic Caption QA and independent private review.
