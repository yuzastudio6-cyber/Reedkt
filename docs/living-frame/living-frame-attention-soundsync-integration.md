# Living Frame Attention, Semantic Scale, Camera, and SoundSync Integration

## Purpose

`living-frame-attention-soundsync-integration-v1` is the read-only,
non-character handoff between an admitted Living Frame scene and the existing
renderer and SoundSync owners. It does not create a second clock, camera,
renderer, audio pipeline, work graph, or approval lane.

The compiler consumes the exact selected-scene publication, owner scope
amendment, execution requirements, canonical MasterTiming binding,
choreography binding, component records, and canonical motion-v3 specs. It
revalidates every digest before emitting the handoff candidate.

## Attention Journey

Each attention event is bound to one canonical semantic phase and its exact
frame range:

- `prepare` uses the prepare phase;
- `handoff` uses activate;
- `hold` uses demonstrate; and
- `restore` or `transition_away` uses resolve.

Focus depth, camera reframe, camera push, light emphasis, and motion emphasis
must resolve to typed motion-v3 tracks. Motion emphasis may be carried by a
changing primary track or by a restrained changing secondary track such as a
shadow response. A handoff must have a later restoration event unless the
scene explicitly transitions away.

`local_contrast` remains a renderer-owned primitive request. The approved style
profile must resolve its exact numerical values. It cannot silently turn into
source blur.

The current Strait of Hormuz regression proves source-focus and restrained
secondary motion emphasis. That selected scene does not contain a virtual
camera track, so it does not by itself prove active camera choreography.
Camera mapping is typed and verified, but a representative active
non-character camera fixture remains required.

## Semantic Scale

Literal physical scale and data-proportional scale remain at unit scale in the
candidate. Perspective or editorial symbolic scale requires a changing scale
track and the exact factual guard already approved for the component. The
handoff never treats symbolic scale as literal geography or data.

## SoundSync Request

Living Frame supplies meaning, not the final sound edit. Each semantic cue
request carries:

- the exact attention event and allowed canonical frame range;
- the linked component;
- purpose, priority, narration protection, and ducking expectation;
- choreography motion-track lineage;
- canonical motion-v3 track lineage; and
- a deterministic preferred hit candidate.

The preferred hit is not final timing authority. Canonical SoundSync must
return:

- exact start, hit, and end frames;
- attack and release envelope;
- approved asset identity, digest, and provenance;
- gain, pan, stereo, room, and reverb treatment;
- narration-ducking automation;
- StoryTiming event lineage;
- approved snapshot, work, asset, and renderer lineage; and
- audio QA and private-review evidence.

Until that response exists, the integration remains pending and cannot make
the active private-internal scope ready.

## Active Scope

The compiler rejects illustrated/living/organic character animation,
complete-character keyposes or interpolation, living-subject rigging, and
mechanical rigging. It also rejects changing layer motion on a primary living
subject or mechanical component. Static illustrations may remain unanimated.

The handoff contains no raw chat, transcript, caption text, audio/media bytes,
paths, URLs, prompts, credentials, commands, or environment values. It grants
no provider, tool-route, dispatch, runtime, asset, work-graph, renderer,
SoundSync, QA approval, private-review, cost, billing, delivery, or production
authority.
