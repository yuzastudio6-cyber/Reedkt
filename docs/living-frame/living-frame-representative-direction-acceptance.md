# Living Frame Representative Direction Acceptance

## Purpose

`living-frame-representative-direction-acceptance-v1` defines the minimum
professional creative-direction evidence required before the twelve active
representative cases may render. It is a source-only internal-test acceptance
profile, not a second planner.

The canonical semantic Living Frame planner remains responsible for selecting,
refining, simplifying, or refusing a scene. MasterTiming and StoryTiming remain
the exact-frame owners, SoundSync remains the exact cue/mix owner, and the
approved renderer remains the final-canvas owner.

## Action-specific direction

Every case has a different five-phase relative timing profile:

```text
prepare → activate → demonstrate → resolve → settle
```

The five positive weights always total 10,000 basis points, but no exact frame
is supplied. A diagram receives a longer comprehension demonstration than its
entrance. An archive receives more assembly time. Hybrid expansion reserves a
deliberate return. Static illustration uses stillness as the primary contrast.
Final-artifact review adds no new motion.

The profile also varies:

- photographic, archival, editorial-cutout, paper-collage, vector, and
  technical treatments;
- flat, shallow 2.5D, and deep-multiplane depth;
- negative-space, speaker-stage, full-scene, evidence-board, exact-diagram,
  and expand/return composition;
- source, environmental, editorial, spatial, camera, and attention motion;
- literal, proportional, perspective, disclosed-symbolic, or no semantic
  scale; and
- minimal, organic, or restrained-documentary sound palettes.

No scene may carry more than one primary and two subordinate meaningful motion
channels. Ambient motion remains subordinate and camera motion must have a
narrative reason.

## Owner scope and QA

Living/organic subject animation and mechanical rigging/part animation remain
forbidden. The static illustrated subject remains static. Exact map/data
geometry cannot be distorted for editorial emphasis. Captions stay above the
Living Frame layer, a focus handoff must restore or intentionally transition
attention, and every case requires the exact 13 professional checks plus
complete playback and complete-time visual review.

Geometry, runtime success, or numeric metrics cannot approve professional
quality. `canonicalConsumptionPending:true`; the profile grants no planner,
timing, sound, work, asset, renderer, QA, review, dispatch, billing, public, or
production authority.

## V2 source-lineage reconciliation

V1 direction profiles predate the semantic source correction and still point to
the v1 visual fixture and v1 case-source admissions. They remain useful as the
frozen action-, style-, depth-, motion-budget-, and relative-phase design
profiles, but they cannot drive representative rendering.

`living-frame-representative-direction-acceptance-v2` is an additive,
source-only reconciliation candidate. It revalidates the exact v1 professional
profile, visual-fixture v2, and source-role-reconciliation v1, then preserves
each case's creative direction while replacing only its source lineage with:

- exact semantically routed v2 source candidate IDs;
- the exact visual-fixture v2 case and route digests;
- the exact source-role-reconciliation case digest; and
- one ordered case-source-admission v2 digest reference that still requires a
  canonical reread.

The Hybrid Expansion and Attention directions now refer directly to approved
topic-matched B-roll. They do not manufacture stills, generate living-subject
motion, or admit character or mechanical rigging. All five relative phase
profiles are preserved, but exact phase frames remain pending decisions of Head
Intelligence and the canonical StoryTiming owner.

V2 explicitly keeps every case non-admissible for runtime. It creates no Head
decision, clock, cue, work item, asset, renderer layer, QA approval, review,
cost event, delivery, or production authority. Canonical consumption and all
case-source-admission rereads remain pending.
