# Living Frame animation-aware illustration private composite

## Scope

Run:

```text
npm run smoke:living-frame-animation-aware-illustration-private-composite-internal-test
```

The test consumes the real modern Musashi-style illustration and the cleaned
straight-alpha artifact from the preceding private alpha test. It creates one
bounded 120-frame, 640 × 360, 30 FPS Living Still scene through the existing
offline Remotion final-composition runtime.

This is private internal execution evidence. It creates no provider dispatch,
customer billing, public delivery, canonical asset-manifest, or production
authority.

## Scene design

The scene uses the approved Living Frame philosophy rather than AI-generated
video:

1. an ink-background plane moves with negative parallax;
2. the cleaned still character remains the visual anchor and receives a
   restrained push plus drift;
3. a separate foreground slash trail reveals, settles, and exits with stronger
   parallax;
4. source blur and luminance perform a smooth Focus Handoff and restoration;
5. Caption Direction remains above every Living Frame layer; and
6. a restrained sword cue is mixed through the existing narration-protected
   supplemental-audio policy.

Remotion remains the final canvas owner. FFmpeg prepares the bounded source,
component raster, audio cue, frame probes, and media measurements. Sharp
renders the approved SVG caption and slash assets through its existing
structured image operation.

## Alpha and destination QA

The exact prepared character overlay is remeasured after landscape placement.
The test requires:

- real alpha variation;
- no blocking alpha finding;
- black, white, mid-gray, and saturated-red composite measurement;
- measurement against the exact destination frame;
- a valid destination edge-contrast result; and
- preserved 640 × 360 straight-alpha output.

The measured destination values for the current frozen fixture are evidence,
not universal thresholds. Canonical asset QA remains the owner of admission
policy.

## Rendered-output QA

The test persists the final MP4 create-only in private Remotion storage,
reopens the exact bytes, and independently verifies:

- H.264 output dimensions, frame count, and rate through FFprobe;
- multiple distinct rendered-frame digests;
- visible character/camera motion;
- visible but temporary slash motion;
- the caption plane above the Living Frame scene;
- the source narration-proxy tone before and during the cue;
- the sword cue absent before and present during its assigned range; and
- narration remaining dominant over the cue.

Three content-addressed private review frames are retained so human visual
review can reject technically valid but creatively poor motion.

## Honest boundary

This proof exercises real animation-aware illustration, postprocessed alpha,
deep multiplane 2.5D composition, attention, captions, sound, persistence, and
rendered QA. The character is still one principal cutout; it does not yet prove
an articulated hair, robe, arm, or sword component rig. That separate
component-decomposition and selective-part-motion proof remains required
before claiming the full character-animation portion of Living Frame is
internally complete.
