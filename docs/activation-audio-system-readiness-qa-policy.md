# Phase 36F Audio System Readiness QA Policy

Mandatory QA gates:

- `evidence_chain`
- `artifact_integrity`
- `source_integrity`
- `plan_snapshot_integrity`
- `audio_metrics`
- `privacy_security`
- `operational_readiness`
- `beta_scope`
- `blocked_features`

Readiness may be true only when Phase 36E artifacts are verified, the beta
scope manifest exists, rollback/fallback is documented, and production,
external beta, paid production, broad real media, RNNoise, Demucs, providers,
Revideo, FILM, and slow motion remain blocked.
