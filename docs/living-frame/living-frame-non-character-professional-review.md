# Living Frame Non-Character Professional Review

## Purpose

Technical success is not professional visual success. The versioned
`living-frame-non-character-professional-review-v1` contract requires Head
Intelligence to inspect every actual non-character Living Frame scene at
playback speed, cover the complete duration, inspect representative entry,
peak, hold, settle, and exit frames, and audition sound when present.

This contract applies to:

- Living A-Roll;
- non-character Living Still;
- Living Archive;
- Living Diagram;
- non-character Hybrid Expansion; and
- deliberate restraint/non-use ranges included in a reviewed scene.

It does not admit character animation or mechanical rigging.
An approved static illustration may remain in the composition, but this review
cannot use animated-character evidence to approve it.

## Required Review Areas

The review checks:

- narrative comprehension;
- focal hierarchy, attention handoff, and restoration;
- source, speaker, contact-object, and product preservation;
- safe zones, captions, labels, and composition;
- depth, occlusion, anchors, perspective, and parallax;
- motion meaning, timing, easing, holds, and settling;
- Semantic Scale, data, geography, and factual integrity;
- maps, diagrams, documents, typography, and labels;
- color, light, texture, grain, and source integration;
- SoundSync restraint and narration protection;
- expansion, return, transition, and scene continuity;
- edges, flicker, compression, and output artifacts; and
- overall professional polish and style coherence.

Mode-irrelevant checks may be marked not applicable with evidence, but the
core comprehension, attention, layout, depth, motion, color, artifact, and
overall-polish checks can never be skipped.

## Dispositions

- `accepted`: technical QA passed and every applicable visual check passed.
- `repair_required`: only local repairable visual failures remain. The repaired
  complete scene must be reinspected.
- `rejected`: technical QA failed or a blocking visual failure requires a
  replan, approved fallback, or scene replacement.

Rejected and repair-required outputs cannot proceed to canonical
reconciliation. Technical metrics, pixel counts, digest checks, successful
encoding, or a green renderer process can never approve visual quality by
themselves.

The review is private evidence only. It creates no selection, layout,
MasterTiming, SoundSync, approved-snapshot, work-graph, asset, renderer, cost,
billing, delivery, or production authority.
