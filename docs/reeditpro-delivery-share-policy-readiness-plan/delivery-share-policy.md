# Delivery Share Policy

Future delivery is private by default. A share or download may be approved only after an approved plan snapshot, completed export state, ready artifact manifest, settled credit ledger state, and audit event are all present.

Blocked by default: public artifacts, permanent unauthenticated URLs, draft-plan shares, delivery before credit settlement, and delivery before artifact manifest readiness.

No delivery endpoint, public artifact, signed URL, storage write, or runtime share path is created in this plan.
